06 / Before you launch

A few good
questions.

Still curious? [ Talk to us ↗ ]

What content can I add?

SupportMate supports PDFs, markdown files, web pages, and images. Add approved content to your workspace for your assistant to use.

How do I put it on my website?

Configure your widget key and allowed domains, then embed the widget script on your website. You can also share a public chatbot URL.

Can I control where the widget runs?

Yes. Use the domain allowlist and widget key settings to control which origins can load your website widget.

How do I improve its answers?

Review conversation history and analytics, update your approved sources, and refetch website content when it changes.

Can I try it before choosing Pro?

Yes. The Free plan includes 10 documents, one website, 10 photos, and 100 messages.
